1.4 WHY NOW? (MARKET TIMING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

✅ CONVERGENCE OF CATALYSTS:

1. AI/ML Explosion in Science
   - AlphaFold solved protein folding (2020-2024)
   - Generative AI for drug discovery booming
   - LLMs being adapted for scientific reasoning
   - Scientists NEED platforms that integrate AI tools

2. Open Source Maturation
   - Scientific Python ecosystem matured (NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, BioPython)
   - WebAssembly enables browser-based heavy computation
   - Containerization (Docker) standardized reproducibility
   - Cloud costs dropped 90% in 5 years

3. Post-COVID Research Acceleration
   - Remote collaboration now normalized
   - $200B+ global increase in R&D spending
   - Digital transformation of labs accelerated by 5 years
   - Government funding at historic highs (CHIPS Act, EU Horizon)

4. Talent Availability
   - Mass exodus from Big Tech (2022-2024 layoffs)
   - Scientists wanting better tools (they're frustrated users too)
   - Remote-first work accepted globally
   - Developer experience expectations raised by GitHub/GitLab/VS Code

❌ WHY THIS DIDN'T WORK BEFORE:
- Cloud was too expensive (2010-2015)
- Browsers couldn't handle computation (pre-WebAssembly)
- Scientific Python wasn't mature enough (pre-2020)
- COVID didn't force remote collaboration yet
- AI wasn't ready for prime time in science

⏰ **THE WINDOW IS NOW.** First-mover advantage in this space is still 
    available, but closing fast.

actual science."

Problem 2: COST BARRIERS
"The tool I need costs $5,000/year. My lab can't afford it. So I use an 
outdated pirated version from 2019, which has bugs that were fixed years ago."

"It took me 3 weeks to install GROMACS with all the right dependencies. And 
when I upgraded my OS, it broke. I cried. Literally cried."

Problem 4: REPRODUCIBILITY CRISIS
"I published a paper in 2023. Last month, someone emailed me saying they
